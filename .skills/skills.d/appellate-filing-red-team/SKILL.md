# appellate-filing-red-team

Use this skill to red-team appellate filings and find procedural defects before submission or after receiving an opponent's filing.

## Rules

- Check every deadline against the relevant court's rules and calendar.
- Verify service was completed on all required parties with proof.
- Confirm the record on appeal includes all designated documents.
- Flag missing appendices, formatting violations, and page limits.
- Never assume a deadline; verify against the actual rule.
- Separate confirmed defects from suspected issues.

## Outputs

- defect report
- deadline compliance checklist
- service verification status
- record designation audit
- formatting compliance check
- prioritized remediation list
